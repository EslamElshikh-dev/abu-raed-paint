Riyadh coverage

Residential and commercial painting across Riyadh

We serve projects in north, east, west, central and south Riyadh, subject to scheduling and project scope.

Typical work includes apartment repainting, villa interiors, living rooms, bedrooms, ceilings, doors, facades, offices, shops and wallpaper installation.

If walls show peeling or damp marks, repainting alone may not solve the issue. Plumbing leaks, waterproofing failures or structural concerns should be handled by the relevant specialist before the affected surface is refinished.

02

03

Final review

Check coverage, edges, corners and cleanliness before the work is considered complete.
